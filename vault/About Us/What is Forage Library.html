What is Forage Library

Forage Library is an open knowledge repository for collecting, organizing, and developing resources that help people understand and navigate the systems shaping human thought, technology, education, and society.

The Library exists because information alone is not knowledge. Knowledge becomes useful when it can be examined, connected, contextualized, and applied.

This repository brings together frameworks, research, references, learning materials, experiments, and working documents developed or curated through Forage.

It is designed as an open infrastructure for learners, educators, researchers, and practitioners to explore ideas without relying solely on institutional gatekeeping or algorithmic recommendation.

The Library seeks to answer the following three questions:

  1. What knowledge, frameworks, and evidence help us understand the systems around us?
  2. How can knowledge be organized so that people can examine, connect, and reuse it?
  3. What can we build when knowledge is treated as shared infrastructure rather than disposable content?
