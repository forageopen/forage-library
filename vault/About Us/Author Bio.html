Adam Rosman

Founder & Chief Learning Officer, Forage

Adam Rosman is a creative entrepreneur, education innovator, and university lecturer working across creative technology, AI, and digital learning. At 27, he founded Forage, an independent research lab exploring AI, data rights, and ethical learning. He also participated in Shell LiveWIRE 2025 as part of a Top 30 shortlisted company.

A former TM R&D intern and international exchange student, Adam brings experience across product design and creative management. He contributes to the open-source community by designing productivity tools and applications, while teaching university courses spanning graphic design, information design, Generative AI, entrepreneurship, and mobile application development.

His work explores how emerging technology can augment human capability and accelerate human progress. His mission is to democratize intelligence by making technology more accessible and improving one's standard of living. Adam believes technology should extend one's mind, foster innovation, build confidence, and foster personal growth.
