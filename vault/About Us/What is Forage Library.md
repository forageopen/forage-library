# What is Forage Library

Forage Library is where I collect the knowledge I've picked up through research, product design, learning and development, creative work, and technology, and try to put it in a form someone else can actually use.

Information has never been the scarce part. There is more of it than anyone could get through. The harder thing is turning it into knowledge: something you can examine, connect to what you already know, place in context, and apply to a real problem in front of you.

So this is less an archive than a workspace. It holds frameworks, research, references, learning materials, experiments, and working documents, some made through Forage and some gathered from elsewhere, kept in a way that invites you to take them apart and reuse them rather than only read them.

I wanted it to stay open. Not sitting behind an institution that decides who gets to learn what, and not filtered through a feed built to hold attention. Just the material, organised well enough that a learner, an educator, a researcher, or someone building on their own can find a way through it.

Underneath, I keep returning to the same three questions: what knowledge, frameworks, and evidence help us understand the systems around us; how that knowledge can be organised so people are able to examine, connect, and reuse it; and what we can build when knowledge is treated as shared infrastructure rather than disposable content.

Muhammad Adam Rosman
