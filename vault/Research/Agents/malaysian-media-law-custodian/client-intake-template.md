# Client intake

The agent collects this before any `assess`, `review`, `matrix`, or `demand`
work. An analysis built on a paraphrase is worthless and dangerous. Mark each
answer **known / partial / unknown**, and each unknown **blocking /
non-blocking** for the specific question the client is asking.

**Not every mode needs every field.**
- `review` (pre-publication) — mandatory: 1 (exact words), 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8a, 9,
  10, 13, 14, 17. The rest are N/A or premature; note them as such.
- `assess` — all of A and B; C and D as relevant.
- `matrix` — all of A and B; the charge details in question 10 are mandatory.
- `demand` — section D is the core; plus 1, 5, 6, 13, 14, and questions 10–12.

## A. The communication (if there is a specific one)

1. **Exact words.** The full, verbatim text. Not a summary. If audio/video: a
   verbatim transcript plus a note of tone, on-screen text, and edits.
2. **Medium.** Platform, format (post / thread / video / livestream / article /
   caption / DM / private group / broadcast), whether public or restricted.
3. **Audience.** Follower count, who actually saw it, whether it was shared or
   picked up elsewhere, language.
4. **Timing.** Date and time posted. Whether it is still up, edited, deleted,
   reposted. Whether anything else was posted around it that reads as context.
5. **Target.** Who or what the statement points at — an idea, a system, a policy,
   an institution, a named person, a protected office (Ruler, judge, prosecutor,
   police, religious authority). Be precise.
6. **The imputation.** In plain words, what a reasonable reader takes the client
   to be *asserting as fact* about that target — as opposed to opinion,
   prediction, or rhetorical question.
7. **Factual basis.** What evidence the client actually holds for any factual
   assertion (documents, data, first-hand knowledge, published sources).
8. **Purpose.** Why the client posted it; what they were responding to.
8a. **Prior public record on this subject.** The client's own posting/publication
   history on this topic and about this target. A prosecutor or opposing counsel
   will read the new item against the old ones — anything there that could
   evidence intent, bad faith, a pattern of conduct, or a communal target needs
   to be on the table now.
9. **Prior contact.** Whether the client had ever contacted the target directly,
   once or repeatedly, before or after (goes to the "harass a person" limb of
   CMA s.233).

## B. Procedural stage — governs everything

10. Which of these is true right now?
    - [ ] Nothing has happened — pre-publication or published-and-quiet
    - [ ] A person or organisation has complained directly to the client
    - [ ] A report has been made to MCMC / police / another authority (known or suspected)
    - [ ] The client has received a letter of demand / cease-and-desist
    - [ ] MCMC or police have contacted the client / requested a statement / seized a device
    - [ ] The client has been interviewed under caution (s.112 CPC statement)
    - [ ] The client has been arrested / released on bail / on a bond
    - [ ] The client has been charged — with what, under which section, which court, next date
    - [ ] Trial underway / concluded / on appeal
11. Any deadline currently running (a demand's response window, a bail condition,
    a court date, a takedown notice)?
12. Is the client already represented by a Malaysian lawyer for this matter? Who,
    and what have they advised?

## C. The client and the operation

13. Individual, or a business / media operation? If a business: entity type,
    who the directors are, who actually published.
14. Does anyone else share exposure — co-author, editor, employer, platform,
    source, the person who filmed or posted it?
15. Does the operation hold personal data of third parties (subscribers, sources,
    customers)? (PDPA flag.)
16. Is any of the content print / film / video distributed publicly? (PPPA /
    Film Censorship flags.)
17. What outcome does the client want — defend a charge, respond to a demand,
    decide whether to publish, reduce ongoing risk, prepare a constitutional
    record, pursue someone who harmed their reputation?

## D. Reputation track (only if the client wants to pursue someone)

18. Exact words of the statement made *about the client*, where published, by
    whom, to whom, when.
19. Whether that statement asserts a fact (e.g. "committed a crime", "was
    convicted") or states an opinion.
20. Whether it was a complaint made to an authority rather than a publication to
    the world — this raises privilege and malice questions that materially change
    a defamation claim, and is for counsel.
21. Concrete reputational or financial damage, with evidence.

## Output of intake

A filled sheet, plus:
- **Blocking unknowns** — the analysis the client asked for cannot be done
  reliably until these are answered.
- **Non-blocking unknowns** — proceed, but caveat the affected sections.
- **Immediate flags** — anything in section B that means "stop, instruct a
  criminal lawyer now, before anything else".
