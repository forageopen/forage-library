Welcome Note

Over the years, through my work in research and development, product design, learning and development, creative work, and technology, I've come across a lot of knowledge that usually takes years to encounter properly. Some of it comes from research, some from working on real projects, and some from simply making enough mistakes to understand why something works the way it does.

I started putting these things together because I wanted to make that knowledge easier to reach. Not by reducing everything into simple answers, but by bringing together the ideas, methods, references, and practical experience that can help someone understand a subject and eventually use it for themselves.

That is what Forage Library is becoming. A place where I can collect and organise the things I've learned, the things I'm still learning, and the things I think are worth passing on. Some of the material here may be useful to someone already working in the field. Some may be useful to someone who has never encountered the subject before.

If something I've spent years learning can eventually help someone understand their first product idea, work through a hobby project, make sense of a research paper, or build something they otherwise would not have known how to build, then the effort is worth it.

Insyallah, maybe one day, some of this knowledge will find its way to a primary school kid working on a hobby project and give them a head start I never had.

Muhammad Adam Rosman
